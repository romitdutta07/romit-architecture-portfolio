About me

Architecture shaped by context, culture, and people.

My work explores how architecture can respond to the emotional and environmental needs of everyday life. I am interested in adaptive reuse, public space, and low-carbon design strategies that bridge tradition and innovation.

Through studio projects and research, I aim to design spaces that are practical, expressive, and rooted in the lived experience of the communities they serve.
